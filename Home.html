ABES NEWS

    ABES News & Updates:

  • Welcome to the ABES News section, your source for the latest updates from ABES Engineering College, Ghaziabad. This page brings together important developments related to academics, admissions, placements, student achievements, campus activities, research, technology, and upcoming events.

    • ABES Begins the 2026 Academic Session:

  • ABES Engineering College has commenced its academic activities for the 2026 session. According to the college's official website, classes for all courses commenced on September 10, 2026. The institution has also published information related to UPTAC physical reporting and admission-related procedures for various programmes.

    • ABES Continues Focus on Industry-Ready Education:

  • ABES is emphasizing practical and industry-oriented learning alongside classroom education. The college currently offers programmes including B.Tech, M.Tech, MCA and MBA, while its academic initiatives increasingly highlight technology, innovation, artificial intelligence and entrepreneurship.

    • Placement Updates:

  • Placements remain an important part of the ABES student experience. The college's published 2026 placement information reports 1,289 placements, 1,870 placement offers, a highest salary package of ₹45 lakh per annum, and a reported median salary of ₹4.50 lakh per annum.

    • Research, Innovation and Startups:

  • ABES has expanded its focus beyond conventional classroom education through research, innovation and entrepreneurship. The college reports more than 2,690 Scopus publications, more than 375 published patents and 54 startups incubated.

    • Important Notices:

  • Students should regularly check the official ABES circulars and notices for information concerning examinations, scholarships, branch changes, academic procedures, admissions and other institutional announcements.
  • Recent official notices include information related to 2026–27 scholarships, branch-change procedures and Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) IDs.

About ABES


At ABES Engineering College, we ignite the flames of curiosity, knowledge, and innovation. Established over two decades ago under the esteemed Society for Educational Excellence, our institution has evolved into a dynamic center of learning nestled in the heart of Ghaziabad. Our unwavering commitment to excellence has garnered us noteworthy positions in the National Institute Ranking Framework (NIRF), a testament to our dedication to quality education.


Beyond engineering, our academic spectrum encompasses a diverse range of programs, including M.Tech, MCA, and MBA courses, designed to cater to the multifaceted aspirations of our students. As we stride forward, our core values of integrity, excellence, and innovation remain at our foundation. ABES Engineering College is not just an institution; it's a journey of transformation, empowerment, and enlightenment. Join us on this extraordinary voyage, where potential finds its path, and dreams take flight.
