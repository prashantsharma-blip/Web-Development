About ABES

ABES Engineering College is located in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh. The institution offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs including B.Tech, BCA, MCA and M.Tech.

ABES focuses on academic learning, innovation, research, industry exposure and student development.
